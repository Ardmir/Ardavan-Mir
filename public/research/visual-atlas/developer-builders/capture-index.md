# Developer Builder Mechanism Audit — public edition

Observed September 3, 2026. This gallery contains 60 exact product states across 13 products, organized through six comparison lenses: scope and delegation, creation and steering, inspection and comparison, governance, handoff, and recovery.

Evidence comes from public first-party documentation, product announcements, embedded demos, and a public training PDF. It is a purposive sample rather than a complete market census. The Temporal image is from a 2023 workshop. Versions, entitlements, reliability, adoption, accessibility, and user outcomes were not independently established by this visual review.

The study retains screenshots and observable product descriptions. Sources are attributed by product; multiple pages may contribute to a product’s reference set.
